# Step 0 — Zee Learn se approval lena (sabse pehle karo)

## Ye kyun zaroori hai

"Kidzee" ek **registered trademark** hai jo Zee Learn Ltd. ka hai. Aap franchisee ho,
matlab aapko brand **use karne ka licence** mila hai — lekin us licence ki shartein
hoti hain. Zyadatar preschool franchise agreements mein ye clause hota hai ki
franchisee brand ke naam se koi bhi publicity material, website ya social media
account **franchisor ki likhit anumati ke bina** nahi banayega.

Iska matlab ye **nahi** hai ki aap website nahi bana sakte. Kidzee ke sekdon
franchisees ki apni websites hain (jaise `kidzeetathawade.com`, `kidzeetexcity.com`,
`kidzeehoramavu.com`, `kidzeekankurgachi.in`). Bas process follow karna hai.

### Agar approval nahi li to kya risk hai
- Zee Learn se legal notice aa sakta hai
- Website ya Instagram/Facebook page delete karne ko kaha ja sakta hai
- Domain par khareedne ka paisa aur mehnat barbaad
- Franchise agreement breach ka issue

### Approval lene se kya faayda hai (sirf risk se bachna nahi)
1. **Official logo files** mil jaayengi (AI/EPS/PNG) — hamesha ke liye
2. **Brand kit** milega — exact colours, fonts, jo bhi aap use kar sakte ho
3. Zee Learn ka **official centre microsite** (jaise `kidzee1234.kidzee.com`) —
   ye SEO mein bahut strong hota hai kyunki `kidzee.com` domain ki authority milti hai
4. Kabhi-kabhi Zee Learn **co-marketing support** ya ready-made creatives deta hai
5. Approved centre ke naate aap official Kidzee centre locator mein aa jaate ho

---

## Kya poochna hai

Apne **Franchise Business Manager (FBM)** / Territory Manager ko email karo. Agar
unka contact nahi hai to Zee Learn ka franchise support number/email use karo.

**Chaar cheezein maango:**
1. Independent website chalane ki permission + domain name approval (`kidzeeiglas.com`)
2. Official logo files aur Brand Guidelines / Brand Kit
3. Aapke centre ka official Kidzee microsite link (agar already bana hai)
4. Facebook / Instagram page ke naam ki approval + naming convention

## Bhejne ke baad

- **Email ka reply save kar lo.** Written approval ka screenshot/PDF safe rakho.
- Agar 5-7 din mein reply na aaye to **phone karke follow up karo**, aur phone par
  jo baat ho uska summary wapas email kar do ("As discussed on call today...").
  Isse written record ban jaata hai.
- Jab tak approval na aaye, website ko **live domain par publish mat karo**.
  Tab tak aap aaram se photos collect karo, content check karo, aur Google
  Business Profile ki tayyari karo (wo brand-neutral kaam hai).

> **Note:** Ye guidance general franchise practice par based hai — ye legal advice
> nahi hai. Apne franchise agreement ka trademark/advertising clause khud padho, ya
> kisi vakil se ek baar dikhwa lo. Har agreement thoda different hota hai.
