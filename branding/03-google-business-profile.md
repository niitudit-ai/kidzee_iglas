# Google Business Profile — sabse zyada faayda dene wala kaam

## Ye kyun sabse pehle?

Iglas mein jab koi parent phone par "play school near me" ya "iglas mein play school"
search karta hai, to Google **Maps ka box** sabse upar dikhata hai — website se bhi
upar. Us box mein aana hi asli jeet hai.

Ye **bilkul free** hai. Aur website se pehle bhi kaam karna shuru kar deta hai.
Schools ke liye ab local search hi primary admission channel ban chuka hai
([reference](https://www.ezrankings.com/blog/local-seo-for-schools/)).

> ⚠️ Business name mein "Kidzee" use karne se pehle Zee Learn se confirm kar lo
> (dekho `01-zee-learn-approval-email.md`). Ye poochhna us email mein already
> shaamil hai.

## Setup — step by step

### Step 1: Profile banao ya claim karo
1. Phone par **Google Maps** kholo → apni location search karo
2. Ho sakta hai profile **already bana** ho (Google khud bana deta hai). Agar
   "Kidzee" naam se kuch dikhe → us par tap karo → **"Claim this business"**
3. Agar kuch nahi hai → [business.google.com](https://business.google.com) par jao
   → "Add your business"

### Step 2: Basic details — bilkul exactly aise
| Field | Kya bharna hai |
|---|---|
| Business name | **Kidzee Iglas** (extra words na daalo — Google penalise karta hai) |
| Primary category | **Preschool** |
| Additional categories | Play School, Kindergarten, Educational Institution |
| Address | Near Pathwari Temple, Gonda Road, Iglas, Aligarh, Uttar Pradesh — PIN 202124 *(PIN code confirm kar lena)* |
| Phone | +91 91933 33793 |
| Website | https://www.kidzeeiglas.com (jab live ho jaaye) |
| Hours | Mon–Sat 8:30 am – 1:30 pm · Sunday Closed |

### Step 3: Pin ko exactly sahi jagah rakho
Map par pin **drag karke apne gate par** le jao. Yahi cheez decide karti hai ki
"near me" search mein aap aayenge ya nahi. Pathwari Temple ke reference se
adjust karo.

### Step 4: Verification
Google postcard bhejega (address par letter) ya video verification maangega.
- Video verification mein: gate ka board, building, andar classroom, aur aap
  khud dikhne chahiye
- Postcard 1-2 hafte mein aata hai — code aayega, usse verify karna hai

### Step 5: Photos daalo (yahan bahut log dhil kar dete hain)
**Minimum 15 photos** upload karo. Jyada photos = jyada trust = jyada calls.

| Type | Kitni | Kya |
|---|---|---|
| Logo | 1 | Official Kidzee logo, square |
| Cover | 1 | Building/gate with board, landscape |
| Exterior | 3 | Gate, building, road se view (parents ko dhoondhne mein aasaani) |
| Interior | 5 | Classrooms, play area, washroom, reception |
| Team | 2 | Director ki photo, teachers ka group photo |
| At work | 4 | Bachche activity karte hue (permission ke baad) |

### Step 6: Description likho (750 characters)
Copy-paste karo:

> Kidzee Iglas is a trusted play school and preschool near Pathwari Temple on Gonda
> Road, Iglas, Aligarh. For over 10 years we have been nurturing children aged 1.5

> every child receives individual attention. Both Hindi and English are used so
> children stay comfortable while gaining confidence. Admissions for the 2026-27
> session are open. Parents are warmly welcome to visit the campus, meet our
> teachers and see the classrooms before deciding. Call 91933 33793.

### Step 7: Attributes on karo
- ✅ "Identifies as women-owned" — agar applicable ho
- ✅ Wheelchair accessible entrance — **sirf agar sach ho**
- ✅ Onsite services
- ✅ Appointment required — "No" rakho (walk-in welcome)

## Reviews — asli game changer

Google par 4.5+ rating aur 20+ reviews wala school baaki sabse aage nikal jaata hai.
Iglas jaise town mein 20 reviews hi bahut hote hain kyunki competition ke paas
5-10 bhi nahi honge.

### Reviews kaise laayein
1. Profile verify hone ke baad Google aapko ek **short review link** deta hai
   (`g.page/r/...`). Usko copy kar lo.
2. Us link ka **QR code** bana lo (free QR generator se) aur reception par chipka do
3. Har **happy parent** ko WhatsApp par ye message bhejo:

> नमस्ते [नाम] जी 🙏
>
> [बच्चे का नाम] को Kidzee Iglas में पढ़ाने का मौका देने के लिए धन्यवाद 😊
>
> अगर आपको हमारा स्कूल पसंद आया हो, तो क्या आप Google पर 1 मिनट का छोटा सा
> review लिख देंगे? इससे इगलास के और माता-पिता को अच्छा स्कूल चुनने में मदद
> मिलेगी।
>
> यहाँ लिखें 👉 [YOUR GOOGLE REVIEW LINK]
>
> बहुत-बहुत धन्यवाद 🙏
> हिमांशु कुमार शर्मा, Kidzee Iglas

**Best timing kab poochna:**
- Annual day / celebration ke turant baad (parents khush hote hain)
- Jab bachche ka koi progress card ya achievement do
- Jab parent khud aakar kuch acha bole — "Sir, aap ye Google par likh denge?"

### Review ke jawab hamesha do
- **Acha review** → 24 ghante mein thank you likho, bachche ka naam le kar
- **Bura review** → gussa nahi. Politely maafi + solution + "aap mujhe 91933 33793
  par call kar lein, main personally dekhta hoon". Public mein bahas mat karo.
  Ek shaant jawab 10 naye parents ko impress karta hai.

## Website ke schema mein coordinates daalna (thoda technical)

Profile ban jaane ke baad:
1. Google Maps par apni location kholo → pin par right-click / long-press
2. Latitude, longitude copy karo (jaise `27.7123, 77.9345`)
3. `index.html` kholo, `"areaServed"` line dhoondho, aur uske **theek pehle** ye
   paste kar do:

```json
  "geo": {
    "@type": "GeoCoordinates",
    "latitude": 27.7123,
    "longitude": 77.9345
  },
```

4. Usi file mein `"sameAs": []` ko aise bhar do:

```json
  "sameAs": [
    "https://www.facebook.com/kidzeeiglas",
    "https://www.instagram.com/kidzee_iglas",
    "https://g.page/YOUR-GOOGLE-PROFILE"
  ],
```

5. Aur **map ko pin-accurate** karne ke liye: Google Maps par apni jagah → Share →
   Embed a map → HTML copy karo → `index.html` mein `<iframe>` ka `src` badal do.

**bilkul same** naam, address aur phone daalo:

- [ ] Justdial
- [ ] Sulekha
- [ ] IndiaMART (education category)
- [ ] Bing Places for Business
- [ ] Apple Business Connect (iPhone Maps ke liye)
- [ ] Facebook Page (address ke saath)
- [ ] SchoolMyKids / Edustoke / SkoolBeep jaise school directories
- [ ] Local Aligarh Facebook groups mein introduce karo

"Kidzee Iglas" aur doosri jagah "Kidzee Play School, Iglas" likha to Google confuse
ho jaata hai aur ranking gir jaati hai.
