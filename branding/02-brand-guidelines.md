## 2. Logo

### Structure
```
K i d z e e     <- wordmark (multi-colour)
─────────       <- yellow rule
I G L A S       <- locality, SAME font, wide letter-spacing
```

### Rules
- **"Kidzee" wordmark ko kabhi mat badlo** — na colour, na font, na stretch.
  Official file hi use karna hai.
- "IGLAS" locality line hamesha wordmark ke **neeche** aayegi, **same font family**
  mein, wide letter-spacing ke saath.
- Logo ke chaaron taraf khaali jagah chhodo — kam se kam "K" ki height jitni.
- Minimum size: print mein 25 mm width, screen par 120 px width. Isse chhota nahi.

### Kya nahi karna
- ❌ Logo ko stretch/squeeze karna
- ❌ Logo par shadow, outline, glow lagana
- ❌ Logo ko busy photo ke upar rakhna (pehle white/solid patti lagao)
- ❌ Logo ke colours badalna
- ❌ Logo ko tirchha (rotate) karna

## 5. Photography style

- **Asli bachche, asli classroom.** Stock photos bilkul mat use karo — local
  parents turant pehchaan jaate hain aur bharosa toot jaata hai.
- Natural daylight. Flash nahi.
- Bachche kuch **karte hue** dikhein, camera ki taraf line mein khade nahi.
- Camera bachche ki aankhon ki height par.
- Background saaf — bags, dustbin, wires hata do.
- Hamesha **parent permission** ke baad hi publish karo (detail:
  `assets/img/README.md`).

## 6. Tone of voice — kaise baat karni hai

Aapka customer ek **maa-baap hai jo pehli baar apne bachche ko ghar se bahar
bhej raha hai.** Wo excited hai aur dara hua bhi. Isi emotion ko address karo.

| Aise likho ✅ | Aise nahi ❌ |
|---|---|
| "Aapka bachcha pehle din roye to ghabraiye mat — hum dheere-dheere ghulate hain." | "We provide holistic pedagogical interventions." |
| "Aaiye, school dekh jaaiye. Koi dabaav nahi." | "Book a consultation with our academic counsellor." |
| "Iglas mein 10 saal se." | "A legacy institution of unparalleled excellence." |

**Principles:**
1. **Simple bhaasha** — jaise aap parent se aamne-saamne baat karte ho
2. **Bachche ke baare mein baat karo, school ke baare mein nahi** —
   "aapka bachcha seekhega" > "hamare paas facilities hain"
3. **Darr door karo** — safety, timing, first-day crying, pickup — inhe address karo
4. **Har baat ke saath ek CTA** — kya karna hai next? Call? WhatsApp? Visit?
5. **Jhooth kabhi nahi.** Jo facility nahi hai wo mat likho. Ek jhooth pakda gaya to
   chhote town mein baat poore Iglas mein phail jaati hai.

### Hindi/Hinglish kab use karein
- **Website:** English default + Hindi toggle (already bana hua hai)
- **Instagram/Facebook captions:** Hinglish sabse best — jaise log bolte hain
- **WhatsApp par parents se:** Hindi
- **Pamphlet/hoarding:** Hindi bada, English chhota

## 8. Ye claims sirf tab karo jab sach ho

Website mein maine kuch general claims likhe hain (safe campus, trained teachers,
small batches, activity-based learning). **Website live karne se pehle poora padho
aur jo aapke centre par lagoo nahi hota use hata do.**

Ye claims **bilkul mat** karo jab tak 100% sach na ho:
- CCTV camera (agar nahi hai to nahi likhna)
- Transport / school van
- Day care / creche facility
- AC classrooms
- Meals / lunch provided
- Koi bhi award ya ranking
- Bachchon ki sankhya (agar exact nahi pata to "hundreds" jaisa likho)

Chhote sheher mein aapka sabse bada asset **bharosa** hai. Ek over-claim usko
kharaab kar sakta hai.
