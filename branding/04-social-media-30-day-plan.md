# Social Media + Branding — 30 Day Launch Plan

## Pehle ye samajh lo: Iglas ke liye priority order

Iglas ek chhota town hai. Yahan Mumbai wali strategy kaam nahi karegi. Aapka
customer WhatsApp aur Facebook par hai, Instagram par thoda kam, LinkedIn par
bilkul nahi.

| # | Channel | Kyun | Time per week |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Google Business Profile** | "play school near me" ka jawab. Sabse zyada calls yahan se aayengi | 15 min |
| 2 | **WhatsApp Business** | Iglas mein admission WhatsApp par hi hota hai, form se nahi | 20 min |
| 3 | **Facebook Page** | 28-42 age ke parents ka main platform tier-3 towns mein | 40 min |
| 4 | **Instagram** | Younger parents + Reels ka reach | 30 min |
| 5 | **Offline** (neeche detail) | Chhote town mein sabse strong. Isko ignore mat karna | 2 ghante |
| 6 | YouTube | Baad mein. Sirf annual day / campus tour video daalne ke liye | — |

**Total: hafte mein ~4 ghante.** Isse zyada mat karo, warna 2 hafte baad chhod
doge. Consistency > perfection.

## Content ke 5 pillars

Har post inme se kisi ek category ka hoga. Sirf admission ads mat daalo — log
follow chhod denge.

| Pillar | % | Kya |
|---|---|---|
| 🎨 **Classroom moments** | 40% | Bachche activity karte hue — asli photos/videos |
| 💡 **Parenting tips** | 20% | Hindi mein useful salah. Ye sabse zyada share hota hai |
| 🏫 **Behind the scenes** | 15% | Teachers, campus, safety, routine |
| ❤️ **Social proof** | 15% | Parent testimonials, bachchon ki achievements |
| 🎓 **Admission CTA** | 10% | Direct admission announcement |

---

## Weekly rhythm (isko yaad kar lo)

| Din | Kya post karna hai |
|---|---|
| **Mon** | Classroom activity — photo ya 15-sec video |
| **Wed** | Parenting tip (Hindi) — carousel ya simple text graphic |
| **Fri** | Facility / teacher / behind-the-scenes |
| **Sat** | Parent testimonial ya bachche ki achievement |
| **Sun** | Admission reminder + CTA |

Roz **1 story** bhi daalo — kuch bhi, 10 second ka. Story se pata chalta hai ki
school "zinda" hai.

## Paid ads — jab thoda budget ho

Pehle 30 din **organic** karo. Uske baad:

**Facebook/Instagram ad (₹200/din se shuru):**
- Objective: **Messages** (WhatsApp par le jaaye) — "Traffic" nahi
- Location: **Iglas + 10 km radius** (Aligarh poora nahi — paisa barbaad hoga)
- Age: **24–40**
- Interests: Parenting, Kids
- Creative: campus ka **video** (photo se behtar chalta hai)
- Budget: ₹200/din × 20 din = ₹4,000/month

Ek admission ki value soch lo — ₹4,000 mein 2-3 admission bhi aa gaye to bahut
faayda hai.

## Aakhri baat — sabse important

**Consistency > perfection.**

Hafte mein 3 simple post jo har hafte aayein, un 10 fancy posts se behtar hain jo
ek baar aayein aur phir 2 mahine kuch na aaye.

Roz subah 10 minute nikaalo:
1. Ek photo/video khinch lo (bas phone se)
2. Ek line caption likh do
3. Post kar do

Bas itna. 6 mahine mein Iglas mein aapka school sabse "visible" school ban jaayega.
