About Us

Iglas Ke Parivaaron Ka 10 Saal Se Bharosa 🤝

Kidzee Iglas sirf school nahi — yahan aapke bacche ki pehli seekh aur pehli dosti banti hai.

10 saal pehle Shri Himanshu Kumar Sharma ji ne ek soch ke saath Kidzee Iglas shuru kiya — ki Iglas ke bacchon ko bhi wahi quality wali shuruaati shiksha mile jo bade shehron mein milti hai. Tab se sekdon bacche yahan se nikal kar bade schools mein confidence ke saath gaye hain.

Hum Kidzee ka hissa hain — Zee Learn Ltd. ka brand, jiske India aur Nepal mein 2000+ centres hain. Matlab hamara curriculum, teacher training aur learning material sab nationally tested hai, aur Iglas ke bacchon ke liye locally dhala gaya hai.

Hamara vaada simple hai: har bacche ko naam se jaana jaaye, roz kuch naya sikhaya jaaye, aur har parent ko yakeen ho ki unka bacha surakshit haathon mein hai. ❤️

FAQs

Parents Ke Aam Sawaal ❓

Kidzee Iglas kahan hai?
Hum Pathwari Mandir ke paas, Gonda Road, Iglas, Aligarh (U.P.) mein hain. Raasta samajhne ke liye 91933 33793 par call karein, ya neeche diye map mein dekhein.
Admission ke liye bacche ki umar kitni honi chahiye?
Lagbhag 1.5 saal se bacche Playgroup mein aa sakte hain. Nursery ~2.5–3.5 saal, LKG ~3.5–4.5 saal aur UKG ~4.5–5.5 saal ke liye hai. Sandeh ho to call karein — hum sahi class bata denge.
Fees kitni hai?
Fees class aur session ke hisaab se alag hoti hai, isliye hum ise phone par saaf-saaf batate hain — poora breakdown, koi chhipa kharch nahi. Aasaan kiston ki suvidha bhi hai. Call: 91933 33793
Admission se pehle school dekh sakte hain?
Bilkul, aur hum chahte hain ki aap zaroor dekhein. Classes, khel ka maidan, washroom — sab dekhiye aur teachers se miliye. Visit free hai aur koi dabaav nahi.
Mera bacha pehle din royega to?
Ye poori tarah normal hai aur hum iske liye taiyar rehte hain. Pehle kuch din hum bacche ko thode-thode samay ke liye bulate hain aur dheere-dheere ghulate hain. Kuch hi din mein bacche khud school aane ko taiyar ho jaate hain 😊
School ka time kya hai?
Somvaar se Shanivaar, subah lagbhag 8:30 se dopahar 1:30 tak. Class ke hisaab se time thoda badal sakta hai, isliye call karke confirm kar lein.
Kidzee doosre play schools se kaise alag hai?
Kidzee India ke sabse bade preschool networks mein se ek hai — 750+ cities mein 2000+ centres. Hamari Péntemind pedagogy NEP 2020 ke anuroop hai aur teachers nationally standardised training leti hain. Matlab — Iglas mein bhi bade shehar jaisi quality.
Day Care ki suvidha hai?
Day Care ke baare mein current jaankari ke liye kripya humein 91933 33793 par call karein — hum aapko samay aur suvidha ki poori detail de denge.
