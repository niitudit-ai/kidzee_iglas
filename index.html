About Us

Trusted by Iglas families for a decade

Kidzee Iglas is not just a school — it is where your child's very first learning memories are made.

A decade ago, Mr. Himanshu Kumar Sharma started Kidzee Iglas with one simple belief — that children in Iglas deserve the same quality of early education that children in big cities receive. Since then we have watched hundreds of children grow from their very first day with us to being confident, school-ready kids.

We are part of Kidzee, one of India's largest preschool networks by Zee Learn Ltd. That means our curriculum, teacher training and learning material are all nationally proven — and then adapted locally for our own children here in Iglas.

Our promise is simple: every child known by name, something new learned every day, and every parent confident that their child is in safe hands.

Our Approach

Kidzee's pedagogy develops five sides of your child at the same time. It is aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP 2020) and India's National Curriculum Framework.

❤️

Empathetic

Understanding others' feelings, sharing and being kind.

🌱

Conscientious

Good habits, discipline and taking responsibility.

🎯

Focused

Paying attention, finishing tasks and staying patient.

🔍

Analytical

Asking questions, comparing and solving problems.

💡

Inventive

Imagining, making things and thinking of new ideas.

Daily Routine

A day at Kidzee Iglas

Children feel safe in routine. Here is how our day flows.

  • 👋 8:30 – 9:00 · Welcome & Prayer A smiling welcome, attendance, a short prayer and settling in.
  • 🎪 9:00 – 9:30 · Circle Time Rhymes, today's weather and date, and letting every child share something.
  • 📚 9:30 – 10:15 · Learning Time Letters, numbers, language and general knowledge — always through activity.
  • 🍎 10:15 – 10:45 · Snack & Rest Washing hands, eating together, and a short rest.
  • 🎨 10:45 – 11:30 · Art & Creativity Colouring, craft, music, dance or dramatic play.
  • ⚽ 11:30 – 12:15 · Play Time Free play, running about and gross-motor activities.
  • 📖 12:15 – 13:00 · Story & Recap A story, a chat about what we learned, and excitement for tomorrow.
  • 🏡 13:00 – 13:30 · Home Time Children are handed over only to a parent or authorised adult.
