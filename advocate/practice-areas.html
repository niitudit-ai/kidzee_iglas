Areas of practice

Family and criminal matters are the areas of focus, but the chamber accepts and appears in the full range of matters heard at the Aligarh district courts. Below is what each area covers in practice.

Family & Matrimonial Law Criminal Law, Bail & Defence Civil Suits & Litigation Property, Partition & Tenancy Cheque Bounce & Money Recovery Motor Accident & Insurance Claims Revenue, Land Records & Tenancy Consumer Protection Service, Labour & Employment Cyber Crime & Online Fraud Drafting, Documentation & Notarial Work Other Matters

Family & Matrimonial Law

Family matters carry consequences that outlast the case itself, which is why they are handled with both care and a clear legal strategy. Proceedings before the Family Court, Aligarh are conducted with an emphasis on protecting children, income and reputation wherever the law permits.

  • Mutual consent divorce (S. 13B, Hindu Marriage Act)
  • Contested divorce — cruelty, desertion, adultery
  • Judicial separation and restitution of conjugal rights
  • Maintenance for wife, children and parents
  • Interim maintenance and enhancement applications
  • Child custody, guardianship and visitation rights
  • Protection orders under the Domestic Violence Act, 2005
  • Dowry harassment matters (S. 498A IPC / S. 85 BNS)
  • Quashing of false matrimonial complaints
  • Court marriage and marriage registration
  • Muslim personal law — talaq, mehr, iddat maintenance
  • Adoption, succession certificates and legal heirship
  • Mediation and out-of-court settlement of family disputes
  • Domestic violence defence for the accused

Criminal Law, Bail & Defence

In a criminal matter the earliest steps decide the range of options that remain later. Applications are prepared and argued under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita and the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, in force since 1 July 2024, as well as the older Codes for matters registered before that date.

  • Anticipatory bail before arrest
  • Regular bail before Magistrate and Sessions Court
  • Bail cancellation and default bail applications
  • Quashing of FIR and criminal proceedings
  • Discharge applications and framing of charge
  • Sessions trial — examination and cross-examination
  • Cheating, forgery and criminal breach of trust
  • Assault, hurt and attempt to murder matters
  • NDPS Act matters
  • SC/ST Act proceedings — complaint and defence
  • Arms Act, Excise Act and Gangster Act matters
  • Complaint cases under S. 156(3) and S. 200
  • Warrants, proclamation and attachment proceedings
  • Criminal appeal, revision and suspension of sentence
  • Compounding and settlement of compoundable offences
  • Preventive proceedings under S. 107/116 and S. 151

Civil Suits & Litigation

Civil disputes are won on documents and on procedure. Suits are drafted with the evidence in mind from the first page, and interim relief is pressed early where a delay would cause irreversible harm.

  • Suit for permanent and temporary injunction
  • Suit for declaration of title
  • Specific performance of agreement to sell
  • Suit for possession and mesne profits
  • Recovery of money and damages
  • Cancellation of sale deed or documents
  • Injunction against illegal construction
  • Written statement and counter-claim
  • Temporary injunction under Order 39 CPC
  • Execution of decree
  • First appeal and second appeal
  • Civil revision and review
  • Caveat and pre-emptive protection
  • Arbitration references and Section 34 objections

Property, Partition & Tenancy

Property matters in and around Aligarh often turn on old records, joint family holdings and undocumented family arrangements. Title is examined thoroughly before any step is advised, because a defect found early is far cheaper to cure than one discovered in court.

  • Partition of joint family and ancestral property
  • Title verification and due diligence before purchase
  • Boundary and encroachment disputes
  • Landlord and tenant disputes, eviction suits
  • Rent arrears and enhancement of rent
  • Illegal possession and forcible dispossession
  • Builder and plot-buyer disputes
  • Family settlement deeds and relinquishment
  • Will, probate and succession disputes
  • Gift deed and its cancellation
  • Easement and right of way matters
  • Property matters of NRIs and outstation owners

Cheque Bounce & Money Recovery

A dishonoured cheque has strict statutory timelines: the notice must go within 30 days of the bank memo, and the complaint within a month of the notice period expiring. Matters are taken up promptly so that a good claim is not lost to limitation.

  • Complaint under Section 138, Negotiable Instruments Act
  • Statutory demand notice within 30 days
  • Defence in cheque bounce prosecution
  • Interim compensation under Section 143A
  • Summary suit for recovery of money
  • Recovery on promissory notes and loan agreements
  • Legal notice for outstanding payment
  • Settlement negotiation and compounding
  • Attachment before judgment
  • Execution and realisation of decretal amount

Motor Accident & Insurance Claims

Compensation in an accident claim depends on how carefully income, dependency and disability are proved. Claims are built on documents from the outset — FIR, charge sheet, medical papers, salary proof and disability certificate — because the tribunal awards on evidence, not on sympathy.

  • Claim petition before the Motor Accident Claims Tribunal
  • Death claim by legal heirs and dependants
  • Permanent and partial disability compensation
  • Hit-and-run and no-fault liability claims
  • Insurance company repudiation and defence
  • Enhancement appeals against low awards
  • Life and health insurance claim rejection
  • Release of vehicle and recovery of documents

Revenue, Land Records & Tenancy

For agricultural and rural holdings around Iglas, Khair, Koil and Atrauli, most disputes begin in the revenue courts rather than the civil court. Matters are pursued before the Tehsildar, SDM and the higher revenue authorities under the Uttar Pradesh Revenue Code, 2006.

  • Mutation of name in revenue records (namantaran)
  • Succession entry after death (varasat)
  • Demarcation and measurement of land (paimaish)
  • Partition of agricultural holdings
  • Correction of errors in khatauni and khasra
  • Consolidation (chakbandi) objections and appeals
  • Removal of illegal occupation of gram sabha land
  • Land use conversion and declaration matters
  • Land acquisition compensation and enhancement
  • Revenue appeal, revision and restoration

Consumer Protection

The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 provides a comparatively quick and low-cost remedy, and a complaint may now be filed where the consumer resides rather than where the seller operates. Complaints are drafted with the correct pecuniary limits and jurisdiction so they are not returned on a technicality.

  • Deficiency in service complaints
  • Defective goods and unfair trade practices
  • Builder delay in possession and refund
  • Insurance claim repudiation
  • Bank, NBFC and loan-related grievances
  • Medical negligence complaints
  • E-commerce and online purchase disputes
  • Electricity, telecom and utility complaints
  • Appeal before the State Consumer Commission

Service, Labour & Employment

Service matters are decided largely on the record of the departmental proceedings, so the reply to a charge sheet or show-cause notice often matters more than the eventual litigation. Assistance is available at both stages.

  • Reply to charge sheet and show-cause notice
  • Departmental enquiry representation
  • Wrongful termination and reinstatement
  • Salary, arrears and increment disputes
  • Pension, gratuity and retirement benefits
  • Promotion and seniority disputes
  • Industrial dispute before the Labour Court
  • Workmen's compensation claims
  • Sexual harassment at workplace (POSH) proceedings
  • Contract labour and wage matters

Cyber Crime & Online Fraud

In an online fraud the first hours decide whether money can be frozen and traced. The correct step is an immediate complaint on the national cybercrime portal along with a written complaint to the cyber cell, followed by the appropriate court proceeding.

  • UPI, net banking and card fraud complaints
  • Investment, loan app and job scam matters
  • Social media harassment and stalking
  • Obscene content, morphed images and sextortion
  • Online defamation and reputation matters
  • Data theft, hacking and account takeover
  • Defence in IT Act prosecutions
  • Takedown requests and content removal

Drafting, Documentation & Notarial Work

Most litigation begins with a document that was drafted carelessly. Deeds and agreements are prepared so that they hold up if they are ever produced in court, and existing drafts can be reviewed before you sign them.

  • Sale deed, agreement to sell and gift deed
  • Will and codicil drafting
  • General and special power of attorney
  • Rent and lease agreements
  • Partnership deed and dissolution deed
  • Family settlement and partition deed
  • Affidavits, undertakings and indemnity bonds
  • Legal notices of every description
  • Name, date of birth and address change affidavits
  • Document review before signing

Other Matters

Several other categories of work are handled regularly. If your matter is not listed here, it is still worth a call — you will at least be told which forum it belongs in and whether it is worth pursuing.

  • Lok Adalat and mediation settlements
  • Anticipatory and preventive legal advice
  • Right to Information (RTI) applications and appeals
  • Police complaints and departmental representations
  • Guardianship and succession certificate petitions
  • Passport, character and police verification issues
  • Municipal, licensing and regulatory matters
  • Arbitration and conciliation proceedings
  • Second opinion on an ongoing case
